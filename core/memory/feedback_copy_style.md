description: No em dashes, en dashes, or hyphens-as-punctuation in user-facing copy; no corporate filler
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

In user-facing copy (buttons, headings, body, toasts, errors, empty
states, email subjects), never use em dashes, en dashes, or
hyphens-as-punctuation. Reword with periods, commas, parentheses, or
colons. No corporate filler ("per our conversation", "I hope this finds
you well"). Just say what you need to say. No emojis in email subjects
(encoding corruption).

**How to apply:** this is a writing-style preference for user-facing
strings, not for code comments or internal docs. Also get the brand name
casing exactly right every time (see CLAUDE.md Brand section for the
project's exact spelling).
